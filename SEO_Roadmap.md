
# AI Gravity Academy - Enterprise SEO & Authority Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the strategic steps to build domain authority, acquire high-quality backlinks, and dominate search results for "AI courses online" and related keywords.

## Phase 1: Technical & On-Page Foundation (Completed)
- [x] **Core Web Vitals**: Optimized for LCP < 2.5s and CLS < 0.1.
- [x] **Metadata**: Implemented keyword-rich titles and descriptions.
- [x] **Structured Data**: Added Schema.org markup for Courses, Organization, and FAQ.
- [x] **Mobile Optimization**: PWA manifesto and responsive design.

## Phase 2: Content Authority (Months 1-3)
### 1. "Pillar Page" Strategy
Create long-form, comprehensive guides (2,000+ words) that serve as definitive resources.
- **Topic A**: "The Complete Guide to Learning AI Without Coding in 2025"
- **Topic B**: "How to Build an Automation Agency from Scratch"
- **Topic C**: "ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini: The Ultimate Comparison for Business"

### 2. High-Velocity Blog
Publish 2-3 high-quality articles per week focusing on long-tail keywords.
- *Examples*: "Best Zapier alternatives", "Make.com tutorial for beginners", "Prompt engineering certification value".

### 3. Case Studies & Success Stories
Publish detailed interviews with students who achieved specific results (e.g., "How John Saved 20 Hours/Week"). These attract links from niche newsletters.

## Phase 3: Backlink Acquisition (Months 3-6)
### 1. Digital PR & Data Studies
- Conduct a survey of 500 professionals on "AI in the Workplace".
- Publish the report with unique charts/data.
- Pitch to tech journalists (TechCrunch, Forbes, Verge) as a primary data source.

### 2. Guest Posting Campaign
Target high-DA sites in the EdTech, Marketing, and SaaS space.
- **Targets**: HubSpot Blog, Zapier Blog, Medium Publications (Towards Data Science), various SaaS blogs.
- **Pitch**: "How AI Automation is Changing HR/Marketing/Sales".

### 3. Education Directories & Listings
Submit AI Gravity Academy to reputable course directories.
- ClassCentral, CourseReport, G2 (Software Reviews), Capterra.

### 4. Podcast Appearances
Pitch Chetan Sharma as a guest on "Future of Work" and "No-Code" podcasts. Show notes provide high-quality backlinks.

## Phase 4: Local & Brand Signals (Ongoing)
- **Google Business Profile**: Optimize for "AI Training [City]" if holding physical workshops.
- **Trustpilot/G2 Reviews**: Actively solicit reviews from graduates. Trust signals improve CTR and conversion.
- **Social Signals**: Share every blog post on LinkedIn and Twitter/X with proper hashtags to drive initial traffic signals.

## Phase 5: Monitoring & Iteration
- **Weekly**: Monitor search rankings for target keywords.
- **Monthly**: Audit backlink profile for toxic links.
- **Quarterly**: Refresh old content with new stats and tools.

---
**Goal**: Achieve Domain Authority (DA) of 40+ within 12 months.
